    Learn Coding: Your First Week

    What to actually expect in your first week of learning to code, and how to get through it.

    10 Aug 2026 Listened 120 times

    Show Notes

    Your first week of learning to code rarely looks like the tutorials promise. This episode covers what to expect instead: setting realistic goals, picking one language and sticking with it, and building a tiny first project rather than bingeing videos without writing any code yourself.

    Key Takeaways

    • Pick one language and stick with it for at least a month
    • Build something small on day one, even if it breaks
    • Consistency beats intensity — 30 minutes daily beats one 5-hour weekend session
    • Feeling lost in week one is normal, and it fades fast
