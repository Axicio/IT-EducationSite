    From Tutorials to Real Projects

    How to break out of tutorial hell and start building things that are actually yours.

    +420 views

    "Tutorial hell" is what happens when following along feels like progress but nothing you can build on your own comes out the other side. It's a comfortable trap — every step is laid out for you, so it never quite feels stuck — but comfort isn't the same as learning.

    Finish the Tutorial, Then Break It

    Once you've completed a tutorial project, go back in and change something on purpose: swap the data source, add a feature that wasn't covered, remove a dependency and rebuild that part yourself. The moment you're solving a problem the tutorial didn't hand you, you're doing real development.

    Start Smaller Than You Think

    The jump from "following a tutorial" to "building my own app" feels huge, so most people skip a step: pick something tiny — a single page that solves one small problem you actually have — and build it without a guide. Small and finished beats big and abandoned.

    Expect to Get Stuck — That's the Signal

    Getting stuck on your own project, then figuring it out by reading docs or searching for the error, is exactly the skill tutorials can't teach you. It's uncomfortable, and it's also the difference between "I followed a tutorial about React" and "I can build with React." Our From Course to Portfolio podcast episode walks through turning that first self-directed project into something you can show off.
